
Reviewer #2: This paper proposes a hydrological modeling approach based on HBV model, in which parameter non stationarity is addressed by identifying changes in climate zone classification. However, the proposed concept has several fundamental flaws, and the manuscript is not well organized in its current form.  
Major comments:

1. The most critical concern is that the hydrological model parameters are not only the functions of climate, and they also largely depend on many watershed geophysical characteristics. [Justify]
2. The justification of the proposed approach through real‑world applications is missing, which is a major weakness. In the current version, the framework appears to be demonstrated primarily through modeling experiments rather than validated against independent observational evidence. Robust real‑world applications are essential to support the proposed methodology. [Validation] 
3. It is unclear whether the HBV model was properly calibrated for each catchment. Detailed calibration procedures and performance metrics should be reported to ensure the credibility of the modeling results. [Add a section]  
4. Identifying changes in climate zone is central to the proposed framework. However, the criteria and methodology used to detect climate zone changes are not clearly described and need to be explicitly stated and justified. [Add a section]  
5. The methodology section is not well organized. It is hard to follow. [Simplify]
6. L11-13 on P27:  How was the conclusion that vulnerability is "significantly underestimated" reached? Based on observed climatic data, did climate zone changes actually occur in the studied catchments? This claim requires stronger evidence and clarification [validation and limitation]
7. How was "uncertainty" quantified? Note that "uncertainty" and "sensitivity" are fundamentally different.  [try to remove it]
8. KDE - simplify and tell why it is being used

Minor comments:  
1) In this paper, "model parameters" and "behavior parameters" are both used. Are they interchangeable? If yes, use consistent term throughout the paper.  
2) L53-56 on P9: need to rephrase for clarity.  
3) L40-42 on P13: Fifty parameters? Fifty behavioral parameter sets?  
4) P17: What is the eq. 6 used for in the proposed modeling approach?  
5) Some figures and flow charts might not be necessary.  
6) Fig. 13: clearly label and explain the x  and y axes  

Reviewer #4: NOT SUITABLE FOR PUBLICATION
